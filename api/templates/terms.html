LoanCentral is a record-keeping tool, not a lender. It keeps a record of loans that members of r/{{ sub }} arrange with each other on Reddit. LoanCentral does not lend, borrow, broker, hold, send or receive money, and it is not a party to any loan.

1. What LoanCentral does

LoanCentral records loan agreements that users make with each other on Reddit: who lent to whom, the amount, dates, repayments and status. It does this through a Reddit bot that reads commands in r/{{ sub }} and through this website. Records are entered by the users involved and by moderators.

2. What LoanCentral does not do

  • It does not lend or borrow money, arrange loans, or match lenders with borrowers.
  • It does not hold, transfer or process any funds or payments.
  • It does not set, suggest or approve interest rates, fees or repayment terms. Anything recorded is what the users agreed between themselves.
  • It does not check anyone's identity, income or ability to repay, and it does not verify that money actually changed hands.
  • It does not collect debts, and it does not report to credit bureaus.
  • It does not give legal, financial or investment advice.
  • It is not a bank, lender, broker, money transmitter, credit reporting agency or other regulated financial business.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Your loans are your own. Every loan is an agreement between the lender and the borrower. You are solely responsible for deciding whether to lend or borrow and on what terms, and for the risk of not being repaid.
  • Follow the law. You are responsible for complying with the laws that apply to you, including any laws on lending, interest rates, licensing, taxes and debt collection.
  • Keep records accurate. Only record loans that really happened, with the real amounts. Don't record fake loans, confirm loans you didn't receive, or mark loans repaid or unpaid untruthfully.
