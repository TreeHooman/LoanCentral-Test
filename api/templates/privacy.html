Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

In short: LoanCentral keeps the loan records of r/{{ sub }} and the minimum needed to run the site. It never asks for bank, card or payment details, doesn't sell data, and has no ads or tracking. LoanCentral is not for anyone under 18.

What we collect

WhatWhere it comes fromWhy
Reddit usernameYour comments and posts on r/{{ sub }}To know who lent and who borrowed
Loan recordsBot commands and the dashboard: amounts, currency, dates, repayments, status, the Reddit post or comment involved, and any repayment terms enteredThis is the record LoanCentral exists to keep
Request postsYour [REQ] posts: the title, amount asked, and linkTo attach a loan to the request it funded
Google account ID and email addressGoogle, when you connect your Google accountTo sign you in. We store Google's account ID and your email address; never your Google password

Page viewsYour use of the dashboard (your username, the page, the time)To see which parts of the site are used. Deleted after 90 days
Technical logsOur servers: IP address, time, page requested, errorsSecurity, preventing abuse (for example rate limits), and fixing problems

LoanCentral does not collect bank, card or payment details, government IDs, addresses or phone numbers as part of normal use. File uploads are currently switched off.

Cookies and your browser

We use one cookie, to keep you signed in. It's needed for the site to work. Some pages also save small preferences in your browser (for example, a tip you dismissed). There are no advertising or analytics cookies and no third-party trackers.

Who can see what

  • Everyone on Reddit: the bot posts summaries in r/{{ sub }}, such as your number of loans and totals repaid, lender stats, and "funded" or "repaid" notices on posts. Reddit comments are public.
  • The other person on a loan: can see that loan's details on the dashboard.
  • Moderators and admins: can see all records, activity history, notes and disputes, so they can moderate.
  • The public website: shows only site-wide totals (number of loans, amount lent, repaid rate, number of lenders), never names or individual loans.

How long we keep it

  • Loan records and activity history: kept as the community's lending record for as long as LoanCentral runs, so that records can't quietly disappear.
  • Page views: 90 days.
  • Sign-in links from $login: expire after 30 minutes and work once.
  • Backups: a rolling 14 days.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
