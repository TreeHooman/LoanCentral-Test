Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What We Collect

LoanCentral collects and stores the following information when you use the dashboard:

  • Reddit username — used to identify your account and link your loan records
  • Loan records — amounts, lenders, borrowers, statuses, and dates as submitted by community members
  • Phone number (optional) — only if you choose to add one for SMS reminders
  • Password hash — a one-way hash of your dashboard password; we never store your password in plain text
  • Last login timestamp — for security purposes

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect email addresses
  • We do not collect financial account information
  • We do not track browsing behaviour outside this dashboard
  • We do not sell data to third parties

SMS Reminders

If you provide a phone number, LoanCentral will send you SMS reminders about your active or unpaid loans. These are transactional notifications, not marketing messages. You can opt out at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any SMS message
  • Removing your phone number from your borrower dashboard

SMS messages are sent via Twilio. Your phone number is shared with Twilio solely for message delivery and is subject to Twilio's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

Loan records are retained for the operation of the community lending system. You may request deletion of your personal data (phone number, password, login history) at any time by contacting a subreddit moderator or using the account deletion option in your dashboard settings.

Data Security

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Database connections use SSL. We do not log or store plaintext passwords or payment information.

Third Parties

LoanCentral uses the following third-party services:

  • Reddit — for optional OAuth login (username only, no other permissions)
  • Twilio — for optional SMS reminders
  • Render — for hosting and database

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact a subreddit moderator or use the data deletion option in your dashboard.

Contact

This dashboard is operated by the moderators of r/loancentral. Contact us via Reddit modmail.
