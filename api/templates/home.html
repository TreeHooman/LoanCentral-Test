How it works

Lenders and borrowers agree terms on Reddit. LoanCentral records what happened, so everyone's history is there for the next loan.

Borrowing

  1. Post a [REQ]For example [REQ] ($150) (#City, ST, USA) (Repay $180) (10/15/2026). The bot replies with your loan history and a request code.
  2. Agree with a lenderTalk it over in the thread. When they offer with $loan, confirm once you've received the money with $confirm.
  3. RepayYour lender records it, and the post is marked repaid. Your record, and your rank, go up.

Lending

  1. Get verifiedThe moderators give trusted lenders the Verified Lender flair. That unlocks the lender commands.
  2. Fund a request$fund REQ-0042 on the post, or record it on the dashboard. The post is marked FUNDED.
  3. Record repayment$paid_with_id or one click on the dashboard. Every loan you've made is in one place.

Bot commands

Comment these on r/{{ sub }}. Every command also works with ! instead of $.

Everyone

$login

Set up or recover your dashboard account. The bot DMs you a link.

$stats u/name

Reddit account check: age, karma and activity.

$logi u/lender

A lender's record: lent, repaid, ongoing.

$help

The full command list.

Lenders

$fund REQ-0042

Fund a request. Add 300USD or CAD if the terms changed.

$loan 100 USD

Offer a loan in a [REQ] thread. It's recorded when the borrower confirms.

$paid_with_id ID 50 USD

Record a repayment you received.

$unpaid ID · $refunded ID

Mark a loan unpaid, or cancel it.

Borrowers

$confirm

Confirm a lender's $loan offer once the money has arrived.

$dispute ID

Something wrong with a loan? Flag it for the moderators.

Lender commands need the Verified Lender flair.

Questions

The things people ask most.

Is LoanCentral a lender?

No. LoanCentral only keeps the record. Lenders and borrowers make their own agreements on Reddit; LoanCentral doesn't broker, fund, hold or process money.

How do I get an account?

Comment $login on r/{{ sub }}. The bot DMs you a one-time link; open it and connect a Google account. After that, sign in with Google.

I lost access to my Google account.

Comment $login again and connect a different Google account. The old one stops working straight away.

How do I become a verified lender?

Message the moderators of r/{{ sub }}. Lender commands unlock once they give you the Verified Lender flair.

A loan on my record is wrong.

Comment $dispute with the loan's ID. The loan is frozen and the moderators review it.

Who can see my loans?

Your history (counts and totals) appears in the bot's replies on Reddit, as it always has. Loan details on the dashboard are visible to you, the other person on the loan, and moderators.

LoanCentral is a record-keeping tool only. It does not broker, fund or process loans.
