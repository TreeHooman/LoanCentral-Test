How a loan works

1. Borrower postsA [REQ] post on the subreddit. The bot replies with their loan history and a request code.
2. Lender fundsAfter agreeing terms on Reddit, the lender records it with $fund or on the dashboard.
3. RepaidThe lender records repayment. The post is marked repaid and both records update.
LoanCentral is a record-keeping tool only. Lenders and borrowers make their own agreements on Reddit; LoanCentral does not broker, fund or process loans. · Terms of Service
