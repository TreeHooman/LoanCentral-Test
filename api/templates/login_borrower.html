Borrower Dashboard Access

How to access your loans

Borrowers receive a personal link by email — there is no password or account to create.

1
Your lender or a mod sends you a link — they do this from the LoanCentral dashboard once your loan is recorded.
2
Check your email — the link arrives from LoanCentral and is valid for 7 days.
3
Click the link — it opens a read-only view of your loan history. You can also download your due dates as a calendar file.
Haven't received a link? Contact the lender who funded your loan and ask them to send one, or reach out to a mod on the subreddit.
