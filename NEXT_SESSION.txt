## Security Constraints (MUST PRESERVE)
- Do NOT trust Reddit flair for permissions. Database remains source of truth.
- Do NOT implement Reddit OAuth yet.
- Do NOT touch production credentials.
- Do NOT make live Reddit API changes.
- Do NOT add debt collection, loan brokering, payment processing, credit scoring, or guarantee language.
- Keep verified lender checks database-driven and fresh where sensitive POST routes need it.
