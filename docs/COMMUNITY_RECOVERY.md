# LoanCentral Community Recovery Discussion

Draft, 2026-09-08. Nothing in this document has been posted to Reddit or sent to
members.

## Owner's Direction

The owner reports declining participation because there are too few lenders.
New-lender recruitment should wait until the upgraded bot and community rebrand
are ready. The release should retain lender-controlled recording with no
borrower confirmation step. The bot currently runs on another computer.

Treat lender shortage as the starting diagnosis, then use aggregate records to
measure its extent. This plan does not propose recruiting new lenders before
release. Existing lenders can help review and test the upgrade.

## What We Can Observe

The public feed includes requests, prearranged posts, and funded/paid labels.
There is activity to work with, but a ranked public snapshot does not establish
growth, lender retention, scam frequency, or funding success rates.
[Public community feed](https://www.reddit.com/r/LoanCentral/).

Lender onboarding currently points readers to a wiki, application form, and
contact instructions. Reviewing that whole journey is more useful than assuming
the application is working because a link exists.
[Lender instructions](https://www.reddit.com/r/LoanCentral/comments/1jxicgp/lenders_read/).

A historical discussion under the unverified-lender warning contains a question
about an unanswered application and a moderator response about limited capacity.
This is a lead to investigate, not proof of a current backlog.
[Warning and discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/LoanCentral/comments/1oe9jaw/mod_do_not_take_loans_from_unverified_lenders/).

## Working Hypothesis

Improve trust and the experience of existing participants first. The dashboard
can make records easier to verify and maintain, but cannot by itself supply
reliable lenders or resolve moderation staffing. Validate this hypothesis with
actual recent activity and member feedback before a promotional relaunch.
The software creates a stronger reason to join and stay; an attractive interface
alone does not guarantee lender participation or growth.

## First Experiments

| Experiment | Concrete change to discuss | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reliable onboarding | One lender application route, an assigned reviewer, a backup reviewer, and a published response expectation the team can meet. | Pending applications and median response time. |
| Clear verification | A current verification lookup explaining what verification does and does not mean; a visible reporting route. | Verification questions and unresolved reports. |
| Consistent procedure | Reflect lender-only recording in the pinned guide, bot reply, and dashboard. Include a worked example. | Requests needing manual correction and abandoned recording attempts. |
| Accurate records | Let participants see their own linked loans, check repayment updates, and raise a correction/dispute with evidence kept private. | Unlinked funded requests and unresolved corrections. |
| Existing lender pilot | Invite a small, willing group through an appropriate community channel; collect feedback on time spent recording a loan. | Returning active lenders and successful complete records. |
| Visible moderation | Explain reporting and appeal steps in neutral language; review the tone of existing warnings so people are willing to report problems. | Report response time and resolved cases. |

These are proposals. No unsolicited messages, automatic bans, rules changes,
funding guarantees, or promotion have been authorized or carried out.

## Suggested First Month

- Week 1: measure the reported lender shortage, audit application and reporting
  routes, and document the lender-only loan lifecycle.
- Week 2: test the release in EU and have pilot users review the wording.
- Week 3: deploy only after release checks pass; offer a limited dashboard pilot
  while preserving the established Reddit command path.
- Week 4: review pilot errors, unresolved cases, and returning participants;
  once release criteria pass, launch the rebrand and open lender applications
  at a pace the reviewers can handle.

This is a proposed sequence, not a promised launch date.

## Rebrand Package To Prepare Before Recruitment

- Keep the LoanCentral name recognizable and make the update concrete: request
  codes, lender-controlled tracking, clearer status history, and a visible
  correction/reporting route.
- Draft a single Start Here guide, separate short lender/borrower instructions,
  and one worked request-to-repayment example. Borrowers are not required to use
  the dashboard to let a lender record a loan.
- Prepare a release announcement showing only tested behavior and explicitly
  describing what verification means. Do not promise loans, repayment, safety,
  returns, or growth.
- Prepare the lender application, eligibility information, assigned reviewers,
  and response process before opening recruitment. Do not loosen verification
  simply to increase lender counts.
- Launch to the existing community first. After it works, use moderator-approved
  promotion where permitted; do not cold-message borrowers/lenders or rely on
  referral spam. The community already prohibits those behaviors.

My proposed positioning: "LoanCentral: clear records, accountable lending."
This is draft wording for discussion, not an approved slogan or a claim of
financial protection. No claim that LoanCentral is unique among lending bots
has been verified.

## Baseline To Collect

Use aggregate, access-controlled records already authorized for community
operations. Do not publish private financial evidence or infer outcomes from
missing data. Report the sample period and missing records alongside metrics.

- Unique lenders recording at least one loan in the last 30 days.
- Open requests, linked funded requests, and time to funding for a defined cohort.
- Due loans with recorded repayment versus unresolved overdue status.
- Lender applications awaiting a response and moderation reports awaiting action.
- Share of new loans linked correctly to a request and original Reddit thread.

Use the data to distinguish too few incoming lenders from existing lenders
leaving or becoming inactive. The release can reduce administrative friction;
retention feedback should determine what else lenders need before recruitment
expands.
